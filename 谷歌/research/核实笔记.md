# 第四期《二十年后的补票——巴菲特为什么出手谷歌》核实笔记（v2.1）

口径：股价为 Yahoo Finance 日线（GOOGL，拆股调整），截至 **2026-11-09 收盘 $288.15**；持股取自 SEC 13F 原件。标「⚠️」的是二手报道或尚未拿到原文的，进入配音前要再核一次。

## 零、口径原则（v2.1 起，全片强制）

**每个关于谷歌的投资判断，都必须由谷歌自身的数据或明确对应谷歌的交易证据支撑。**

- 可以用：13F 里 Alphabet 那一行（股数、市值）；10-Q 前五大持仓名单里点名 Alphabet 的句子；Alphabet 自己的公告、8-K、424B5、10-Q、财报；巴菲特、阿贝尔、芒格**谈谷歌**的原话；Alphabet 的股价。
- 不能用：伯克希尔**整个股票组合**的买入额、卖出额、成本、未实现收益、“工商及其他”类别的成本变化等。这些数字混合了苹果、美国银行等所有持仓，不能推出谷歌仓位的任何变化。
  - 反例（v2.0 原 c6a，已删）：“定增之后，伯克希尔没有再大举加仓：整个三季度，它一共只买了约 24 亿美元的股票，卖出的还略多一些。”——统计对象错（组合数据当成谷歌数据）；推论也不成立（组合净卖出时，完全可能一边卖苹果、一边买谷歌）。
- 13F 只披露季末持股，两期之差只能说“净增 / 净减”，不能说成“买了 / 卖了”多少。
- 三季度谷歌持股变化：只有三季度 13F（11 月 16 日前公布）能回答，公布前片中不做判断。

## 一、巴菲特、芒格、阿贝尔关于谷歌的全部公开言论（按时间）

### 2004 年：谷歌上市信里的巴菲特
- 谷歌上市招股书（S-1）里的创始人信《谷歌股东手册》（An Owner's Manual for Google's Shareholders），开头一句 “Google is not a conventional company.”
- 脚注 1 原文：“Much of this was inspired by Warren Buffett's essays in his annual reports and his 'An Owner's Manual' to Berkshire Hathaway shareholders.”（这封信很多内容受巴菲特年报文章和他写给伯克希尔股东的《股东手册》启发）
- 信中谈双重股权结构时写：“The Berkshire Hathaway company has applied the same structure, with similar beneficial effects.”
- 来源：[谷歌 2004 年 S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000119312504073639/ds1.htm)
- 巴菲特在 2004 年股东大会上回应：喜欢这封信的文字，很高兴有人愿意这样坦率地和股东说话；并开玩笑说应该收版税。⚠️ 只见二手报道（CNN Money 2004-05-01，原页面暂时打不开），片中只做转述，不做引语卡。

### 2017-05-06 股东大会：「我搞砸了」
- 巴菲特：“We were their customer very early on with GEICO, for example.” 回忆 GEICO 当时每次点击付给谷歌约 10 到 11 美元：“any time you're paying somebody 10 or 11 bucks every time somebody just punches a little thing”；他认识谷歌创始人，也有机会了解这家公司，“But I blew it.”
- 芒格：“if you ask me, in retrospect, what was our worst mistake in the tech field,” “I think we were smart enough to figure out Google. Those ads worked so much better in the early days than anything else.” 并说伯克希尔在这件事上辜负了股东。
- 来源：[CNBC 巴菲特档案：2017 年股东大会上午场](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2017/05/06/morning-session---2017-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting.html)、[CNBC 2017-05-06](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/05/06/warren-buffett-admits-he-made-a-mistake-on-google.html)

### 2018-05-05 股东大会（已找到 CNBC 编辑版文字稿原文）
- 来源：[CNBC 编辑版文字稿 PDF](https://s3.amazonaws.com/static.contentres.com/media/documents/e3ab342f-baae-465d-a5d3-41a789b624cb.pdf)、[CNBC 巴菲特档案：2018 年下午场](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2018/05/05/afternoon-session--2018-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting.html)
- 原文一（谈 AltaVista 与增量成本）：“Bill Gates told me early on — you know, that I think I was on AltaVista and he suggested I turn to Google. But the trouble is I saw that Google was skipping past AltaVista, and then I wondered if anybody could skip past Google. And I saw at GEICO that we were paying a lot of money for something that cost them nothing incrementally. We've looked at it, and you know, I made a mistake in not being able to come to a conclusion where I really felt that at the present prices that the prospects were far better than the prices indicated.”
- 原文二（三人来访、竞争才是谜）：“at GEICO we were paying them a lot of money at the time they went public. And all three of the main characters — Eric [Schmidt] and Larry [Page] and Sergey [Brin] — they actually came and saw me. But they were more interested in talking about going public and the mechanics of it and various things along that line. But it wasn't like what they were doing was a mystery to me. The mystery was how much competition would come along, and how effective they would be, and whether it would be a game where four or five people were slugging it out without making as much money as they could if one company dominated.”
- 原文三：“But I made the wrong decision on Google.”（单数，紧接着单独谈亚马逊；媒体常写成 “wrong decisions on Google and Amazon”，片中不用这个合并版）
- 来访时间文字稿没说具体日期，只说“谷歌上市那阵子”；有二手文章说在上市前、也有说上市后，片中只说“亲自来见过他”。
- 以下为旧版二手记录，保留备查：
- 巴菲特：“I made the wrong decisions on Google and Amazon.” 并提到是比尔·盖茨劝他把搜索引擎从 AltaVista 换成谷歌。⚠️ 来自媒体报道（[Fortune 2018-05-06](https://fortune.com/2018/05/06/why-warren-buffet-was-wrong-about-google-amazon/)、SCMP），用前对照 CNBC 档案逐字核。
- CNBC 2026 年转述：巴菲特 2018 年说过，虽然通过 GEICO 看到了谷歌广告的威力，但当时没把握谷歌会是长期赢家。⚠️ 属转述。

### 2019-05-04 股东大会

We atone.” 巴菲特接话：他是说 “We blew it.” ⚠️ 来自媒体报道（[Business Standard/PTI 2019-05-05](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/buffett-partner-says-ashamed-for-missing-google-boat-119050500061_1.html)、Bloomberg），用前对照录像。

### 2026-07-15 CNBC：巴菲特本人回应（最重要的一手原话）
- “I initiated it, but I normally wouldn't give you that answer on something like that.”（是我发起的，不过这种事我一般不会回答）
- 关于阿贝尔：“But he is the decider.” “I am not doing anything that [Abel] doesn't approve of. He's not doing anything I don't approve of.”
- 排名：“it's probably number five or six.” “I would say that I don't like it as well as at least four or five other businesses that we own.”
- 认错：“I made a mistake.”（指他过去把谷歌看成轻资产生意）
- 胜算：“more likely to be a winner, based on their record, than probably 90% or 95%” of what Wall Street promotes.
- 最大的疑问：“The real question with Google, and all of its competitors now, is they're all laying out hundreds of billions.” “They weren't playing that game with computer software.” 竞争对手 “don't have any choice”，“playing a game, in many cases, that they don't want to play.”
- 好生意的标准：“The trick in investing is to find businesses that are going to earn high returns on capital” for an extended period of time；“a good business is one that earns a lot more than the returns on essentially riskless investments.” 举美国运通：“it earns 30% plus on capital.”
