# 第四期《二十年后的补票——巴菲特为什么出手谷歌》核实笔记

口径：股价为 Yahoo Finance 日线（GOOGL，拆股调整），截至 **2026-11-09 收盘 $288.15**；持股取自 SEC 13F 原件。标「⚠️」的是二手报道或尚未拿到原文的，进入配音前要再核一次。

## 一、巴菲特、芒格、阿贝尔关于谷歌的全部公开言论（按时间）

### 2004 年：谷歌上市信里的巴菲特
- 谷歌上市招股书（S-1）里的创始人信《谷歌股东手册》（An Owner's Manual for Google's Shareholders），开头一句 “Google is not a conventional company.”
- 脚注 1 原文：“Much of this was inspired by Warren Buffett's essays in his annual reports and his 'An Owner's Manual' to Berkshire Hathaway shareholders.”（这封信很多内容受巴菲特年报文章和他写给伯克希尔股东的《股东手册》启发）
- 信中谈双重股权结构时写：“The Berkshire Hathaway company has applied the same structure, with similar beneficial effects.”
- 来源：[谷歌 2004 年 S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1288776/000119312504073639/ds1.htm)
- 巴菲特在 2004 年股东大会上回应：喜欢这封信的文字，很高兴有人愿意这样坦率地和股东说话；并开玩笑说应该收版税。⚠️ 只见二手报道（CNN Money 2004-05-01，原页面暂时打不开），片中只做转述，不做引语卡。

### 2017-05-06 股东大会：「我搞砸了」
- 巴菲特：“We were their customer very early on with GEICO, for example.” 回忆 GEICO 当时每次点击付给谷歌约 10 到 11 美元：“any time you're paying somebody 10 or 11 bucks every time somebody just punches a little thing”；他认识谷歌创始人，也有机会了解这家公司，“But I blew it.”
- 芒格：“if you ask me, in retrospect, what was our worst mistake in the tech field,” “I think we were smart enough to figure out Google. Those ads worked so much better in the early days than anything else.” 并说伯克希尔在这件事上辜负了股东。
- 来源：[CNBC 巴菲特档案：2017 年股东大会上午场](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/2017/05/06/morning-session---2017-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting.html)、[CNBC 2017-05-06](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/05/06/warren-buffett-admits-he-made-a-mistake-on-google.html)

### 2018-05-05 股东大会
- 巴菲特：“I made the wrong decisions on Google and Amazon.” 并提到是比尔·盖茨劝他把搜索引擎从 AltaVista 换成谷歌。⚠️ 来自媒体报道（[Fortune 2018-05-06](https://fortune.com/2018/05/06/why-warren-buffet-was-wrong-about-google-amazon/)、SCMP），用前对照 CNBC 档案逐字核。
- CNBC 2026 年转述：巴菲特 2018 年说过，虽然通过 GEICO 看到了谷歌广告的威力，但当时没把握谷歌会是长期赢家。⚠️ 属转述。

### 2019-05-04 股东大会
- 芒格说，伯克希尔在 GEICO 身上就看到了谷歌广告的价值，却 “just sat there sucking our thumbs”（坐在那儿吮手指），“We screwed up.” “So we're ashamed. We atone.” 巴菲特接话：他是说 “We blew it.” ⚠️ 来自媒体报道（[Business Standard/PTI 2019-05-05](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/buffett-partner-says-ashamed-for-missing-google-boat-119050500061_1.html)、Bloomberg），用前对照录像。

### 2025-11-14：第一次出现在持仓报告里
- 伯克希尔 2025 年三季度 13F：Alphabet A 股 **17,846,142 股**，季末市值 **$43.38 亿**。同一份报告里继续减持苹果。这是巴菲特卸任 CEO 前最后一份 13F。
- 当时外界不知道是谁买的：[Fortune 2025-11-15](https://fortune.com/2025/11/15/warren-buffett-stocks-berkshire-hathaway-13f-alphabet-shares-ai-bubble/) 写“不清楚是巴菲特、阿贝尔还是其他人做的决定”。

### 2026-06-01：100 亿美元定向增发
- Alphabet 公告：“Alphabet has reached an agreement to sell $10 billion of stock to Berkshire Hathaway Inc. in a private placement”；A 股 $50 亿、每股 $351.81，C 股 $50 亿、每股 $348.20；“This investment by Berkshire Hathaway adds to the position it has built since Q3 2025.”
- 8-K：伯克希尔关联方买入 A 股 14,212,035 股、C 股 14,359,656 股，2026-06-04 完成。资金用于 AI 基础设施和算力；本轮股权融资合计 $847.5 亿（含 $400 亿“随行就市”增发与公开发行）。
- 来源：[Alphabet FWP 2026-06-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000119312526251733/d160205dfwp.htm)、[Claims Journal 2026-06-03](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/06/03/337913.htm)

### 2026-07-15 CNBC：巴菲特本人回应（最重要的一手原话）
- “I initiated it, but I normally wouldn't give you that answer on something like that.”（是我发起的，不过这种事我一般不会回答）
- 关于阿贝尔：“But he is the decider.” “I am not doing anything that [Abel] doesn't approve of. He's not doing anything I don't approve of.”
- 排名：“it's probably number five or six.” “I would say that I don't like it as well as at least four or five other businesses that we own.”
- 认错：“I made a mistake.”（指他过去把谷歌看成轻资产生意）
- 胜算：“more likely to be a winner, based on their record, than probably 90% or 95%” of what Wall Street promotes.
- 最大的疑问：“The real question with Google, and all of its competitors now, is they're all laying out hundreds of billions.” “They weren't playing that game with computer software.” 竞争对手 “don't have any choice”，“playing a game, in many cases, that they don't want to play.”
- 好生意的标准：“The trick in investing is to find businesses that are going to earn high returns on capital” for an extended period of time；“a good business is one that earns a lot more than the returns on essentially riskless investments.” 举美国运通：“it earns 30% plus on capital.”
- 来源：[CNBC 2026-07-15 报道](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/warren-buffett-tells-cnbc-he-initiated-berkshire-hathaways-investment-in-alphabet.html)、[CNBC 摘录](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/15/cnbc-exclusive-excerpts-berkshire-hathaway-chairman-warren-buffett-speaks-with-cnbcs-becky-quick-on-cnbcs-squawk-box-today.html)、[CNBC 2026-07-18](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/18/two-unexpected-revelations-in-warren-buffetts-cnbc-interview.html)、[Steady Compounding 全文整理](https://steadycompounding.com/transcript/warren-buffett-cnbc-interview-july-2026/) ⚠️ 第三方全文，与 CNBC 视频核对后使用

### 2026-09-02 CNBC：阿贝尔讲定向增发的来龙去脉
- “Warren initiated that probably close to 15 months ago or a little bit more.”
- “I received a call on a Sunday morning to see if we wanted to participate in their upcoming equity offering.” 对方建议考虑 “10 billion and more”。
- “I called Warren and I said, we had a significant opportunity to invest in, continued to invest in Google … with a significant block.”
- “Warren and I discussed the size. We discussed the size of discount, and I'd recommended 6.5 percent discount.”
- 为什么喜欢：“We have a lot of visibility from within our companies as to how we're using A.I., what type of benefits it's delivering.” “we saw Google as a significant player.”
- 来源：[CNBC 阿贝尔访谈文字稿 2026-09-02](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/cnbc-exclusive-transcript-berkshire-hathaway-ceo-greg-abel-speaks-with-cnbcs-squawk-box-today.html)

## 三、我自己算的数（估算，片中注明）

- GOOGL 收盘：2017-05-05（说“我搞砸了”前一天）$47.51；2025 年三季度日均 $209.46（区间 $174.36–$254.72）；2026-01–03 日均 $314.21；2026-04–06 日均 $359.96；最高 2026-05-13 $402.62；2026-11-09 $288.15。
- 从说“搞砸了”到真正买入：股价约涨了 **4.4 倍**（47.5 → 209）。
- 估算成本：17.85 万股 × 209 ≈ $37 亿；+4,000 万股 × 314 ≈ $126 亿；+1,957 万股 × 360 ≈ $70 亿；+定增 $100 亿 → 合计约 **$333 亿**。按 11 月 9 日价格，10,598 万股约值 **$305 亿**，账面约亏 8%（C 股略低于 A 股，未细分）。
- 定向增发 A 股成本 $351.81，比 11 月 9 日的 $288.15 高约 18%。

## 五、片中要守住的边界

- 2025 年第一笔是巴菲特本人发起（本人与阿贝尔两处原话互证）；2026 年的加仓和定增由阿贝尔拍板、与巴菲特商量。片中按这个分工讲，不说“巴菲特一个人买了 380 亿”。
- 巴菲特对谷歌的态度是“喜欢，但不是最喜欢”，还明确提出资本开支的疑问。片子不能讲成“股神全力看好”。
- 账面盈亏属估算，按播出前最新收盘价更新。
