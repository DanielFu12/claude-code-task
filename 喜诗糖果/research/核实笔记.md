# 第五期《喜诗糖果》核实笔记（v1）

口径：所有喜诗数字取自伯克希尔致股东信原文（berkshirehathaway.com/letters，原文已存 `research/raw/`，摘录见 `research/sees_passages*.txt`）。
信里不同年份的说法有出入时，下面都写明出处，片中只用标注为“片中口径”的那一个。

## 零、口径原则

- **税前 / 税后**必须写清。1972 年的“约 200 万”是税后、“420 万”是税前；2007 年的 8200 万、累计 13.5 亿、累计 19 亿都是税前。
- **“当年的数”和“累计”分开**：2007 年一年的销售额是 3.83 亿美元、税前利润 8200 万；13.5 亿是 1972–2007 的累计税前利润；19 亿是 1972–2014 的累计税前利润。
- **2014 年以后伯克希尔不再单独披露喜诗的利润**，片中任何“现在”的利润数字都不能说；只说“到 2014 年，累计税前利润 19 亿美元”。
- **买的是 Blue Chip Stamps（蓝筹印花）**，不是伯克希尔直接买：1972 年由伯克希尔与巴菲特、芒格控制的蓝筹印花买下，后来蓝筹印花并入伯克希尔（1983 年完成合并）。片中说“巴菲特和芒格控制的蓝筹印花公司买下喜诗”，或简化为“巴菲特和芒格买下喜诗”时，第一次出现要讲清楚。
- **价格**：卖方名义要价 4000 万，其中含 1000 万多余现金，所以实际要价 3000 万（1991 信）；2007、2014 信都直接说“要 3000 万”。片中口径：**要价 3000 万，出价 2500 万，成交 2500 万**。

## 二、它到底好在哪：看不见的资产

- 1983 信附录《商誉及其摊销》：“It was not the fair market value of the inventories, receivables or fixed assets that produced the premium rates of return. Rather it was a combination of intangible assets, particularly a pervasive favorable reputation with consumers based upon countless pleasant experiences they have had with both product and personnel.”
- 2014 信：“the company had a huge asset that did not appear on its balance sheet: a broad and durable competitive advantage that gave it significant pricing power.”
- 1991 信：“In our See's purchase, Charlie and I had one important insight: We saw that the business had untapped pricing power.”
- 2007 信：两个因素让它几乎不需要资金——“the product was sold for cash, and that eliminated accounts receivable… the production and distribution cycle was short, which minimized inventories.”
- 1983 信：顾客偏好 “we believe most lovers of chocolate prefer it to candy costing two or three times as much. (In candy, as in stocks, price and value can differ; price is what you give, value is what you get.)”
- 1983 信：门店全部直营（not by franchisees），每年要雇约 2000 名季节工；“Cheerful, helpful personnel are as much a trademark of See's as is the logo on the box.”
- 1983 信：“We regard product quality as sacred.”（原料一律买最好的）

## 三、定价权：1972–1984 表格（1984 信原表，片中主图）

| 年份 | 销售额 | 税后营业利润 | 售出磅数 | 年底门店 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1972 | $31,337,000 | $2,083,000 | 16,954,000 | 167 |
| 1975 | $50,492,000 | $5,132,000 | 19,134,000 | 172 |
| 1978 | $73,653,000 | $6,178,000 | 22,407,000 | 182 |
| 1981 | $112,578,000 | $10,779,000 | 24,052,000 | 199 |
| 1984 | $135,946,000 | $13,380,000 | 24,759,000 | 214 |

推算（本片计算，标“按原表推算”）：
- 每磅售价：1972 年 31,337,000 ÷ 16,954,000 ≈ **1.85 美元**；1984 年信中原话 “per-pound realization was $5.49”。约 **3 倍**。
- 同期销量（磅）：16.95 → 24.76 百万磅，**+46%**。
- 同期美国 CPI-U 年均值：1972 年 41.8，1984 年 103.9（BLS），约 **2.5 倍**。所以售价涨幅高于通胀，但不是“随便涨”：1984 年只涨了 1.4%（“we increased prices considerably less than has been our practice in recent years: per-pound realization was $5.49, up only 1.4% from 1983”）。
- 2007：销售额 3.83 亿、3100 万磅 → 每磅约 12.35 美元（本片推算）；CPI 2007 年 207.342，相对 1972 约 5.0 倍；售价约 6.7 倍。
- 来源：BLS CPI-U 历史表（https://www.bls.gov/cpi/tables/historical-cpi-u-201710.pdf）。

定价权的边界（必须同时讲）：
- 1983 信：同店磅数连续下降，“The poundage volume in our retail stores has been virtually unchanged each year for the past four, despite small increases every year in the number of shops… Of course, dollar volume has increased because we have raised prices significantly.”
- 1985 信：“industry trends are not good… We now are willing to increase prices only modestly and, unless we can stabilize per-shop poundage, profit margins will narrow.”
- 1987 信：1988 年提价后销量疲弱（“To date, sales have been weak”）。
- 1990 信：伊拉克入侵科威特后商场客流下降，圣诞磅数略降，但提价 5% 使金额增长。
- 1991 信：近 80% 销售来自加州；加州开征 7%–8.5% 零食税。

## 四、季节与顾客

- 1984 信：“In the four weeks prior to Christmas, we do 40% of the year's volume and earn about 75% of the year's profits.”
- 1987 信：“In 1987, about 85% of our profit was earned during December.” 1991 信：最后两个月通常贡献 80% 以上年利润。
- 2001 信：喜诗夏季亏损不少（“which loses considerable money during the summer months”）。
- 1987 信：删掉 14 款糖，其中两款顾客强烈抗议，“we received many hundreds of letters”，原信引了顾客来信（“A pox on all in See's who participated in the abominable decision...”）。
- 1990 信：阿尔伯克基门店房东不续约，店长安·菲尔金斯（Ann Filkins）发动顾客，263 位顾客写信打电话，房东让步；哈金斯给每人写信致谢、送礼券，并登报列出 263 个名字。
- 1989 信：广告费从 400 万增到 500 万，报纸广告支出增加两倍多，当年磅数增长 8%。
- 2020 信：“When a business manufactures and distributes a non-essential consumer product, the customer is the boss. And, after 100 years, the customer's message to Berkshire remains clear: 'Don't mess with my candy.'”

## 五、印钞机：钱去了哪里

- 2007 信（“Businesses – The Great, the Good and the Gruesome”）：
  - “Let's look at the prototype of a dream business, our own See's Candy.”
  - 行业：“Per-capita consumption in the U.S. is extremely low and doesn't grow. Many once-important brands have disappeared, and only three companies have earned more than token profits over the last forty years.” 喜诗大约占全行业利润的近一半（巴菲特的估计：“I believe that See's … accounts for nearly half of the entire industry's earnings”）。
  - 1972 年 1600 万磅 → 2007 年 3100 万磅，“a growth rate of only 2% annually”。
  - 2007 年：销售 3.83 亿美元，税前利润 8200 万；经营所需资本 4000 万；“we have had to reinvest only $32 million since 1972”；1972–2007 累计税前利润 **13.5 亿美元**，除 3200 万外全部交给伯克希尔（早年是蓝筹印花），交税后拿去买别的好生意。
  - “Just as Adam and Eve kick-started an activity that led to six billion humans, See's has given birth to multiple new streams of cash for us.”
  - 对比：“Typically, companies that increase their earnings from $5 million to $82 million require, say, $400 million or so of capital investment to finance their growth.”（巴菲特举的假设例子，不是统计数）
  - “It's far better to have an ever-increasing stream of earnings with virtually no major capital requirements. Ask Microsoft or Google.”（与第四期呼应）
  - 三种“储蓄账户”：“The great one pays an extraordinarily high interest rate that will rise as the years pass. The good one pays an attractive rate of interest that will be earned also on deposits that are added. Finally, the gruesome account both pays an inadequate interest rate and requires you to keep adding money at those disappointing returns.” 好的例子是 FlightSafety，糟糕的例子是航空业。
- 1991 信：20 年里税前利润中 4.1 亿交给蓝筹印花 / 伯克希尔；净资产只从 700 万增加到 2500 万。
- 2010 信：“take money we earn from, say, See's Candies or Business Wire … and use it as part of the stake we needed to buy BNSF.”（片中可以说“喜诗赚的钱，也是后来买下 BNSF 铁路的本钱之一”，不能说“喜诗买下了 BNSF”）
- 2011 信：2011 年税前利润创纪录 8300 万，累计 16.5 亿；年底账面占用资本（扣除现金）“less than zero”（季节性，圣诞后最低）。
- 2014 信：**到 2014 年累计税前利润 19 亿美元，增长只需追加投资 4000 万**；“(Envision rabbits breeding.)”；“through watching See's in action, I gained a business education about the value of powerful brands that opened my eyes to many other profitable investments.”
- 2014 信：想把钱投回糖果业务扩张，“our many attempts to do so were largely futile”。
- 1997 信：《布法罗新闻报》、喜诗和斯科特·费泽三家合计已付给伯克希尔 18 亿美元。（片中不用合计数）

## 六、它改变了巴菲特

- 1989 信《头二十五年的错误》：“It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price. Charlie understood this early; I was a slow learner.”
- 2012 信：“More than 50 years ago, Charlie told me that it was far better to buy a wonderful business at a fair price than to buy a fair business at a wonderful price.”
- 1991 信：“ownership of See's has taught us much about the evaluation of franchises. We've made significant money in certain common stocks because of the lessons we learned at See's.” —— 信里没有点名是哪只股票。**片中只能说“这些股票里，包括后来的可口可乐吗？他没点名”这类保守说法，或者干脆不连到可口可乐**。v1 口径：说“他说，靠在喜诗学到的东西，后来在一些股票上赚了大钱”；屏幕上可以并列伯克希尔 1988 年起买入可口可乐（系列第一期），但旁白不说“因为喜诗才买可口可乐”。
- 2011 信：“'Buy commodities, sell brands' has long been a formula for business success. It has produced enormous and sustained profits for Coca-Cola since 1886 and Wrigley since 1891. On a smaller scale, we have enjoyed good fortune with this approach at See's Candy since we purchased it 40 years ago.” （这句把可口可乐和喜诗放在同一个道理里，可以用）
- 1983 信附录：会计商誉每年摊销，经济商誉却在涨：“while accounting Goodwill regularly decreased from the moment of purchase, economic Goodwill increased in irregular but very substantial fashion.” 通胀对照：同样赚 200 万，喜诗只要 800 万有形资产，普通企业要 1800 万；通胀翻倍时，喜诗只需再投 800 万，普通企业要 1800 万。
- 2011 信：喜诗与可口可乐、IBM 一样，能在通胀中保持购买力，同时几乎不需新资本；“people will be willing to exchange a couple of minutes of their daily labor for a Coca-Cola or some See's peanut brittle.”

## 六之二、“芒格把我扳了过来”（2014 信，50 周年回顾，本期主线）

- 小标题原文：“Charlie Straightens Me Out”。
- “My cigar-butt strategy worked very well while I was managing small sums. Indeed, the many dozens of free puffs I obtained in the 1950s made that decade by far the best of my life for both relative and absolute investment performance.”
- “Most of my gains in those early years, though, came from investments in mediocre companies that traded at bargain prices. Ben Graham had taught me that technique, and it worked.”
- “But a major weakness in this approach gradually became apparent: Cigar-butt investing was scalable only to a point. With large sums, it would never work well.”
- “It took Charlie Munger to break my cigar-butt habits and set the course for building a business that could combine huge size with satisfactory profits.”
- 1959 年两人在奥马哈经一位医生介绍认识，巴菲特 28 岁、芒格 35 岁。
- “When we differ, Charlie usually ends the conversation by saying: 'Warren, think it over and you'll agree with me because you're smart and I'm right.'”
- “The blueprint he gave me was simple: Forget what you know about buying fair businesses at wonderful prices; instead, buy wonderful businesses at fair prices.”
- “Altering my behavior is not an easy task (ask my family)… But Charlie never tired of repeating his maxims about business and investing to me, and his logic was irrefutable. Consequently, Berkshire has been built to Charlie's blueprint.”
- 1989 信对“烟蒂”法的定义：“A cigar butt found on the street that has only one puff left in it may not offer much of a smoke, but the 'bargain purchase' will make that puff all profit.” 以及 “Unless you are a liquidator, that kind of approach to buying businesses is foolish.”
- 片中口径：说“芒格让巴菲特改掉了捡烟蒂的习惯”，这是巴菲特本人的原话；喜诗是这条路上**第一笔按“好生意、合理价”逻辑买下的大交易**——这一说法信中没有原话，**片中不说“第一笔”**，只说“1972 年的喜诗，是这条路上的一块里程碑”也不说；只按时间讲：1972 年买喜诗时巴菲特仍犹豫，2014 年他把功劳记给芒格。

## 七、边界与反面（客观性，片中必须有）

1. 体量小：行业几乎不增长；销量 35 年年均约 2%。
2. 扩张屡试屡败：2014 信 “our many attempts to do so were largely futile”。所以喜诗的价值在于“把钱交出来”，靠伯克希尔拿去再投资；单独上市的话，利润要分给股东再投资，会被税和摩擦成本吃掉（2014 信原意）。
3. 定价权有边界：1980 年代同店磅数连年下滑；1985 年说只敢小幅提价；1988 年提价后销量疲弱。
4. 集中：近 80% 销售在加州（1991）；加州大地震会同时打击喜诗（1994、1996 信）。
5. 季节性：利润集中在圣诞前；夏季亏损。
6. 2014 年后不再单独披露利润，片中不推测近况。

## 八、伯克希尔股东大会上的喜诗（片尾可用）

- 2022 信：两天里喜诗在股东大会卖出 11 吨（“eleven tons”）花生脆和巧克力，成交额 400,309 美元；周六“about 10 sales per minute”；“selling products that haven't been materially altered in 101 years. What worked for See's in the days of Henry Ford's model T works now.”
- 2015 信：股东大会上巴菲特和芒格吃可口可乐、喜诗软糖和花生脆。

## 九、待核 / 不用

- 1998 年佛罗里达大学演讲里“圣诞节后第二天涨价”之类说法：只见二手文字稿，片中不用。

- 喜诗现价（每磅多少钱）：本期不用，避免引用无出处的零售价。
